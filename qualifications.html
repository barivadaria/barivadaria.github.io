Why Computer Science

Diving into Computer Science headfirst wasn’t easy, I had to pick up coding, data structures, and algorithms in weeks. It was rough, but building from nothing hooked me fast. Since then I’ve tried web dev, embedded systems, and cybersecurity, but what really stuck was tackling messy problems and turning them into something that works.

One of my toughest projects, The Tesseract, is my attempt to fix how recruiting works. Not keyword filters, but something smarter. It’s been long nights, broken code, and plenty of rewrites, but it pushed me further than anything else I’ve built. And that’s the part I love: the grind, the puzzle, and the feeling when it finally clicks.
